# Product intent

## Problem

SMEs need to understand quickly what JoeWork automates, why it is credible, and what action to take next. Generic AI language or unclear offers reduce trust and qualified conversions.

## Primary users

- SME founders and operators in Latin America and the US
- Operations, sales, and customer-service leaders evaluating AI automation
- Prospects comparing an agency-led implementation with generic AI tools

## Jobs to be done

- Understand JoeWork's offer and relevant use cases
- Estimate the operational value and implementation fit
- Verify trust signals, terms, and contact paths
- Start a qualified conversation with JoeWork

## Success criteria

- The core offer and target customer are clear above the fold
- Primary calls to action lead to a working contact or qualification flow
- Use cases describe measurable business outcomes
- Pages are responsive, accessible, indexable, and fast
- Analytics can be enabled through documented environment variables

## Product constraints

- Public content must not expose customer or system secrets
- Claims, pricing, legal language, and contact destinations require explicit source material
- The site must support static export and GitHub Pages
- Spanish and English expansion should not create duplicated, drifting sources of truth

## Open decisions

- Canonical conversion event and target funnel
- Canonical language and localization strategy
- Analytics provider and required consent behavior
- Ownership and review cadence for legal content
