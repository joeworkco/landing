# Privacy Policy

At JoeWork.co, we are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we collect and use information. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices and how we use only public data to improve your experience with our AI automation services.

## Our Data Philosophy

**JoeWork only uses publicly available data.** We do not collect, store, or process any private or sensitive personal information. Our approach is designed to respect your privacy while providing you with powerful AI automation tools.

## Information We Collect

### Public Information Only
We work exclusively with information that is:
- Publicly available on the internet
- Already accessible through public APIs
- Shared by you voluntarily in public forums or platforms
- Available through legitimate public data sources

### Website Analytics
We collect basic website usage information to improve our service:
- Page views and navigation patterns
- Device and browser information
- General geographic location (country/region level)
- Referral sources

This data is aggregated and anonymized and cannot be used to identify individuals.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- **Improve our AI models**: Using only public data to train and enhance our automation capabilities
- **Provide better service**: Understanding how users interact with our platform to improve user experience
- **Ensure system reliability**: Monitoring performance and preventing abuse
- **Customer support**: Responding to inquiries and providing assistance

## What We Don't Do

- **No private data collection**: We do not access, collect, or store any private or personal information
- **No data selling**: We never sell your information to third parties
- **No invasive tracking**: We don't use tracking technologies that compromise your privacy
- **No unauthorized access**: We only work with data that is publicly available and legally accessible

## Data Security

Even though we only work with public data, we implement appropriate security measures to protect all information:
- Secure data transmission using encryption
- Regular security audits and updates
- Limited access controls for our team members
- Compliance with industry security standards

## Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services for:
- Website analytics (Google Analytics)
- Payment processing
- Email communications
- Cloud infrastructure

These services have their own privacy policies and security measures that we carefully evaluate.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Know what public information we may be using
- Request information about our data practices
- Opt out of website analytics cookies
- Contact us with any privacy concerns

## International Users

Our services are primarily designed for users in Latin America (LATAM), but we welcome users from around the world. We comply with applicable privacy laws in the jurisdictions where we operate.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify users of any significant changes through our website or email communications.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

- Email: privacy@joework.co
- Website: [Contact Form](https://joework.co/contact)

We are committed to addressing any privacy concerns promptly and transparently.
